# Employee Handbook
Fictional data for RAG evaluation.

## Working hours
Employees work Monday–Friday, from 09:00 to 18:00.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week.
Manager approval is required.

## Travel expenses
Client-visit travel expenses are reimbursed at actual cost,
up to THB 500 per day. Receipts are required.

## Annual leave
Employees receive 12 days of annual leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least seven days in advance.
