# Demo company handbook

ข้อมูลสมมติสำหรับฝึกเท่านั้น / Fictional training data.

## Working hours
Employees work Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00.
Remote work is allowed two days per week.

## Travel expenses
Client-visit travel expenses are reimbursed at actual cost, up to THB 500 per day. Receipts are required.

## IT support
Report laptop issues to the IT help desk. This handbook does not specify a response time.
